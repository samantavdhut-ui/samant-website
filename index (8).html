About Dr. Atmaram

Six years of research
in your service

I'm Dr. Atmaram Pandurang Samant, a horticulturist with a PhD in Fruit Science from Dr. Balasaheb Sawant Konkan Krishi Vidyapeeth, Dapoli. My research has focused on mango orchard management, crop regulation, flowering physiology, fertigation optimisation, and varietal characterisation — with six publications in NAAS-rated international journals.

With expertise spanning all fruit crops, vegetables, spices, and plantation crops grown in Konkan and Goa, my advice covers the full breadth of what farmers in this region grow — grounded in both rigorous research and hands-on field experience.
